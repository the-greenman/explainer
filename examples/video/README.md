# Video example: decision records (first real-media run, issue semanticops.com#30)

Two real talking-head clips, compiled to ONE file (`decision-records.mp4`) with `intro` and `purpose` as `in`/`out` cuts of it, with overlay cues, captions, a held choice, and rate/reverse/scrub controls. This is the first time the media sync paths ran against files. They work; numbers below.

Run: `sh prepare.sh` once (re-encodes the `demo/video/` sources with a keyframe every 0.5 s, extracts the embedded subtitles to WebVTT, then concatenates with `-c copy` into `decision-records.mp4` plus one `decision-records.vtt` with the purpose cues shifted by 38.6 s; outputs are git-ignored), then `npx vite` and open `/examples/video/`. Measure: `npx vite --port 5199 --strictPort &` then `node examples/video/check.mjs [shotDir]` (headless Chromium via the playwright install in CLAUDE.md; prints the table below and saves screenshots). Unit tests: `node --test examples/video/`.

## What is in it
- `manifest.json`: segments `intro` (in 0, out 38.6) and `purpose` (in 38.6, out 87.3, `ends: stop`), both `src` the one file, markers `start`, `four-questions` (intro 15), `purpose`, `record` (purpose 20.4). Cues:
  - intro `lower-third` title at 1.0-6.6 (left, above the native captions, clear of the face).
  - the four questions as a numbered-list `panel` (right), items timed to speech (`at` = 0.1, 4.5, 5.7, 7.5 s from cue start, i.e. 15.1, 19.5, 20.7, 22.5), up until 28.2.
  - `cycle` diagram (local component, `components.ts`) on purpose 1.3-16: Debate, Decide?, Forget, Repeat; arcs draw in with `p`.
  - `emphasis` (local): clear / visible / useful at purpose 40.2, 41.0, 41.8. **These times are estimated from the line's word pacing (15 words over 5.9 s in the VTT), not checked by listening.**
  - `choice` (variant `scrim`) with `hold: true`, 47.0-48.6, `loop_from: 47.0`. Options: Watch again (goes_to `start`), Replay the four questions (goes_to `four-questions`), Jump to the decision record (goes_to `record` and sets `revisit=yes`, which reveals a gated lower-third at purpose 20.6-26).
- **Cut values.** `in`/`out` are 38.6 / 87.3, not 38.621. In the combined file the audio of purpose starts at exactly 38.600 (ffprobe packet pts), the video's first frame of purpose at 38.621 and the whole video stream is shifted by 0.021 s (file `start_time` 0.021, the AAC priming offset), intro included. 38.6 keeps the segment-relative cue times and the shifted captions (purpose cues + 38.6) aligned with the audio and with the sources; the video lags by 21 ms (under a frame) throughout, uniformly in both segments. Evidence: the keyframe at 38.621 and audio packet at 38.600 in `ffprobe -show_entries packet`; measured at the cut, first purpose frame has mediaTime 38.655 when the clock reads purpose ~0.03.
- Core additions (small): intro variant `lower-third`; numbered-list variant `panel` and optional per-item `at`; choice variant `scrim`. All named variants, no free-form params. Tests in `test/purity.test.ts` and `components.test.ts`.
- Layout rule found by looking at the frames: the speaker's face fills x 27-70% of the frame and native captions are bottom-centre, so overlays live in the left/right 27% columns. Cards are translucent dark with white text so they read over a white wall and the painting.

## Bugs found and fixed in core
1. **Stale caption after a segment swap** (`src/player.ts` `loadSegment`). Removing the old `<track>` while one of its cues was displayed left that cue painted over the new video ("And when to revisit?" from intro under the purpose captions). Fix: set old tracks to `mode = 'disabled'` before `replaceChildren()`. Evidence: screenshot before the fix vs after (`shot-purpose-8s.png`).
2. Trap rather than core bug: default `choice` buttons take `--explainer-bg` / `--explainer-ink`; on a dark page theme they rendered black on black over video. The `scrim` variant sets its own colours.

## Findings and open issues
- **Boundary gap**: separate files freeze about 316 ms locally (measured 2026-10-08; the earlier 120 ms figure undercounted). Decision: one file per explainer, segments as cuts; the cut is then seamless (above). The `ponytail:` in `player.ts` records that a second preloaded element stays in reserve for multi-file explainers. Over R2 a separate file would add network latency on top. Still untested on a real network.
- Reverse is correct but not smooth: a stream of superseded seeks (about 40 frames/s presented; the picture can lag the clock by a frame). Fine with 0.5 s keyframes; 2.2 s originals will be worse (not measured).
- History is now the path taken (continuations and choices). Reverse unwinds a branch when it crosses where the branch landed, so it retraces only what was watched. `back()` re-holds the choice. The ordering caveat: navigation (markers, scroll) never pushes, so after jumping around `back()` returns along the path, not along where the viewer has been.
- The hold loop wraps with a seek, so there is a small hitch on every loop.
- Not verified: Safari/Firefox, phones, real network or R2, audio audibility (headless), the captions on/off button (written, not driven), `play="enter|scrub"` with video, emphasis word timing against the voice. Scrub input is dispatched as an `input` event rather than a mouse drag.
- Path view (`<explainer-path>`, crumbs under the player, tree in a side panel; stacks under the player below 760 px). Driven in headless Chromium: across the cut gives `Why decisions need recording > Avoiding the same conversation twice ▸ 0:01`; at the hold the choice joins as `<> What next?`; key 2 marks "Replay the four questions" taken and nests `The four questions @0:15 ▸ 0:15` under it with the other two greyed; reversing past intro 15 removes the branch (history 2 to 1); option 3 then clicking the choice in the tree re-holds it (purpose 48.6, held, history 1) and key 1 works; clicking the first span goes to intro 0 with history 0. 13 `explainer:path` events over the run, 0 console errors. Found while looking: a button is an atomic inline box, so a long label dropped below its bullet until the tree rows became flex; and the example's `--explainer-ink: #111` (for light cards on video) is unreadable on the dark page, hence the optional `--explainer-path-ink`. Not verified: keyboard focus order by hand, screen reader output (separators are aria-hidden, "now" has no live region), phones.

## Playthrough (default path)
"Watch again" is `default: true`, so with playthrough on the explainer plays through and loops until stopped. The page has a "Playthrough: on/off" button (`data-explainer-action="playthrough"`). `node examples/video/check.mjs` section `h` measures it (headless Chromium, 2x from purpose 45, rAF-sampled):
- Hold passed without stopping: choice wrapper hidden on all 291 frames, 0 frames with a hold, clock reached 48.575 then the entry `{purpose 48.6 -> intro 0, hold: next, option: again, auto: true}`; landed intro 0.000, playing, video-clock diff 0.000; from 0.5 s on, `|currentTime - (in + t)|` max 0.001 over 83 frames, still playing at the end.
- Pitfall (same-segment default, "skip" made default: purpose 48.6 -> 20.4): media followed, diff 0.000 at the first frame after the jump and max 0.000 from 0.5 s on (83 frames). Without a `jump` hook the media would stay at 48.6 and the clock would read it back; the clock now fires `hooks.jump` on every auto branch and the player seeks.
- Toggle off before the hold (button click): holds at 48.600, not playing, wrapper visible, 3 option buttons, history 0. Toggle on while holding (button): intro, playing, video diff 0.035 0.7 s later, entry `auto`.
- Reverse after the same-segment auto branch: unwinds at 48.599 (history 0), still playing at rate -1 and at 48.000 0.6 s later (video diff 0.000).
- Path tree text shows "Jump to the decision record (default)" (and "Watch again (default)" for the loop).
- Not verified: the loop over many laps in a browser (clock unit test only), `back()` in playthrough in a browser (unit tests only), a cross-segment default over a network, no `explainer:choice` event is emitted for auto branches (the `explainer:path` event still fires).

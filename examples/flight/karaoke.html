A karaoke marker

A ball hops through the animation, landing on each concept as the narration says it (the times come from karaoke.vtt with phraseTimes, here in the page script because this example has no build step; a site does it at build time). The concept lights up through data-marker-here and --marker-p. After the last phrase the ball falls out of the video onto the block below, which lights up through data-flight-here. Scrub back and it goes back. Rendered offline, the ball stays in the video and the flight does nothing.

Page block: the ball lands here when the narration says "carry on".

The other way

The ball starts on the page, falls into the video as the narration begins, and from then on is the marker: it hops through the concepts. (Rendered offline, the ball is in the video from the start, resting on the first concept.)
